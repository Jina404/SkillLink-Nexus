# SkillLink Nexus pricing model (internal)

INTERNAL ONLY. Not part of the website. Do not link to this file, move it into `public/`, or publish any of these figures on the site.

## Split
- Contractors receive 80% of the base project price.
- The client pays the base price plus a client-side fee of 8% to 10%. The fee covers payment protection, quality checks and the guarantee.
- SkillLink's total take is about 27% to 30%.

## Price formula
1. Base price = contractor pay ÷ 0.8
2. Client price = base price + client fee (8% to 10%)

## Packages and quotes
- Fixed-price packages with published "from" prices.
- Anything else gets a custom quote within 48 hours.
- Test price points with about 10 real buyers before publishing.

## Payments
- Milestone payments: 50% upfront, held by SkillLink. 50% on client approval.
- Contractors are paid within a couple of days of approval.

## Monthly retainers
- For content, maintenance and reporting.
